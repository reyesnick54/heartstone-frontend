Register as an employer

Register your business so you can legally employ staff and make employer contributions. You need to do this before your first employee starts work.

Labour Department · Service version 2026.2 · Information checked 12 September 2026

Who this is for

  • Companies and sole traders who will employ at least one person
  • People applying on behalf of a business they are authorised to represent
Not covered by this service Employing household domestic workers. Use Register a domestic employer instead.

What you'll need

  • Certificate of incorporation or business registrationA clear scan or photo of the whole document
    Required
  • Proof of business addressUtility bill or lease from the last 3 months
    Required
  • Details of the people you plan to employJob roles and start dates. Names can be added later.
    Required

What happens after you apply

  1. 1
    Documents checkedWe check that everything needed is there. If something is missing, we'll ask you.
    About 2 working days
  2. 2
    ReviewAn officer reviews your application.
    About 5 working days
  3. 3
    DecisionYou'll get the decision and the reasons in your messages.
    About 3 working days

Times are estimates, not guarantees.

Fees

Registration fee[AMOUNT]

You pay after you submit. Paying the fee does not mean your registration is approved.

What you get

Employer registration certificateSaved to your credentials and certificates
Valid until cancelled

Other offices involved

Social Security is told about your registration so that you can start making contributions. You don't need to apply to them separately.

If you disagree with a decision

You can ask the Labour Department to reconsider within 30 days of the decision. If you still disagree, you can request an independent review.
