Public website — design reference

Static design pages for the public HeartStone website: the signed-out front door that leads people to the right service and into their account. Open any page and click through: links between pages work, and "Sign in" continues into the citizen dashboard designs. Sample content only.

Not production code. Every page is responsive (checked at 1440px, 390px and 360px with no sideways scrolling); the canvas shows a phone view of each. Text in [SQUARE BRACKETS] is a placeholder for a real value. Pages marked draft need legal or accessibility sign-off before publishing.
